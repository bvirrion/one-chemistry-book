\begin{solution}{exo:g8:combustion-and-fuels:9}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:combustion-and-fuels:12}
At first there is enough oxygen: \ce{CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O}. As the
oxygen of the closed room is used up, the flame burns short of \omterm{def:g4:air-a-mixture-of-gases:air}{air}:
\ce{2CH4 + 3O2 -> 2CO + 4H2O}. Carbon monoxide, odourless and toxic,
builds up in the room.
\end{solution}

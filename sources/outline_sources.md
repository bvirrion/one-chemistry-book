## What the check changed in the draft

- **Added** Year 2 *Continuous Reactors and Industrial Processes* (PC 2022).
- **Split** atomic structure between Year 1 (quantum numbers, configurations,
  periodicity: PCSI/L1) and Year 2 (atomic orbitals as wavefunctions: PC 2022).
- **Added** alkynes to Year 1 electrophilic additions (Paris-Saclay L3, all L1s).
- **Added** Year 3 *Supramolecular Chemistry* (Strasbourg option, Sorbonne
  biosensors), *Radicals, Carbenes and Rearrangements* (Paris-Saclay synthesis),
  *Environmental Chemistry and Toxicology* (Sorbonne, Paris-Saclay).
- **Added** greenhouse gases (grade 8) and corrosion (grade 9) from cycle 4.
- **Kept** Hückel theory and the Wittig reaction from the older PC programme
  and the licence plans (both absent from PC 2022).
- **Left to physics**: states of matter, density, radioactive decay,
  nucleosynthesis (told only), radiochemistry.
